About Us

A trusted name in Lahore property rentals

SJ Real Estate & Builders is a real estate consultancy and property brokerage based at Akram Park, Ghalib Market, Gulberg III, Lahore. The business is owned and operated by Sharjeel Ayub and is formally registered.

Our main focus is residential and commercial rentals across the leading residential and business districts of Lahore. We lease independent houses, modern flats and apartments, as well as shops, offices and retail spaces that fit the real needs of tenants and property owners.

Through honest dealing, accurate local market valuation and steady follow-up with every client, we have built a strong network of landlords, corporate clients and residential tenants across the city.
